# Security Policy

Security is part of what we build — identity verification, banking onboarding and backup supervision all run on Th1nkDev code. We take every report seriously and appreciate responsible disclosure.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue, discussion or pull request for a security problem.**

Use one of these private channels:

1. **GitHub private vulnerability reporting** — on the affected repository, go to **Security → Report a vulnerability**, when it is enabled.
2. **Email** — [contact@th1nkdev.com](mailto:contact@th1nkdev.com) with the subject line `[SECURITY] <product or domain>`.

Please include:

- The affected product, URL or repository, and version or commit if known
- A description of the issue and its potential impact
- Step-by-step reproduction, proof of concept or screenshots
- Your name or handle if you would like to be credited

## What to expect

- We acknowledge your report within **3 business days**.
- We investigate, keep you informed of progress, and tell you when the issue is fixed.
- We ask that you give us reasonable time to fix the issue before any public disclosure, and we will agree on a disclosure date with you.
- With your permission, we credit you once the fix is released.

## Scope

In scope: products and services built and operated by Th1nkDev, including `th1nkdev.com` and its subdomains, and code in the [th1nkdev-io](https://github.com/th1nkdev-io) organisation.

Out of scope: systems owned and operated by our clients (report to them directly — we will help route reports if you are unsure), third-party services, and findings that require physical access or social engineering of our staff.

## Safe harbour

We will not pursue legal action against anyone who, in good faith:

- reports a vulnerability through the channels above,
- avoids privacy violations, data destruction and service disruption,
- accesses only the data needed to demonstrate the issue and does not keep it,
- does not run denial-of-service, spam or automated high-volume scanning against production.
